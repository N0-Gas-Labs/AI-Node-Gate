# Commission a Platform Blueprint

## What this is

No_Gas_Labs™ is accepting a limited number of platform-blueprint commissions.

Bring one written idea, problem, community need, service concept, public-interest project,
or digital-platform question.

Damien Featherstone, the Neophyte Founder of No Gas Labs, will direct an
AI-assisted research and design process to turn it into a decision-ready blueprint.

## What you receive

A platform blueprint can include:

- The problem statement and target users
- User roles and permissions
- The central user journey
- Core features and a first-version feature cut
- Screen and workflow descriptions
- Data/entities the platform would need
- Assumptions, unknowns, and risks
- Comparable platforms and differentiation
- A staged build plan
- A plain-language brief suitable for developers, collaborators, funders, or advisors

## What this is not

This is not a promise that No_Gas_Labs™ will build, host, operate, market,
fund, or maintain the resulting platform.

The output is a research-and-design artifact. The commissioner retains responsibility
for decisions and for any future implementation.

## Request a commission

Open a GitHub issue using the **Platform Commission Request** template.

In your request, include:

1. The problem you want a platform to address
2. Who would use it
3. What success would look like
4. What you already know
5. What you do not know
6. Whether the result should be public or private
7. Your desired turnaround
8. Your proposed budget or compensation structure

No_Gas_Labs™ will reply with one of:

- Accepted
- Needs clarification
- Not a fit
- Referred / deferred
