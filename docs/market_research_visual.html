Feature → revenue roadmap (by impact per effort)

  • Tier 1 — unlock enterprise: non-custodial + hardware-backed signing · compliance-evidence packs (EU AI Act / ISO 42001 / SOC 2) · SSO/RBAC/policy.
  • Tier 2 — scale: replicated/HA ledger + signed checkpoints · air-gapped appliance kit · compliance onboarding services.
  • Tier 3 — moat: coordination intelligence (routing, dependency graphs, confidence) · federation across hubs · optional managed tier.
  • Tier 4 — distribution: LangGraph/CrewAI adapters, ServiceNow/Jira bridges, SIEM export · certification & partner program.

Recommended path to profitability

  • Open-core, self-hosted-first. Free, complete core; monetize enterprise assurances.
  • Beachhead: regulated self-hosted AI — government/defense and BFSI first.
  • Wedge: “human signature on every AI decision, proof on your own hardware.”
  • Flagship feature: non-custodial, hardware-backed signing — biggest risk, biggest revenue.
  • Budget-line conversion: compliance-evidence packs turn the ledger into a must-buy.
  • Target revenue mix: ~50% Sovereign · ~25% Business · ~15% services · ~10% OEM/certification.
