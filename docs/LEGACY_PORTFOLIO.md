# Legacy Portfolio — the prior world, remembered

> **Status: historical memory.** This directory is the compressed memory of the
> organization that preceded this hub (`No-Gas-Labs-Official`, 94 repositories).
> It is preserved so that doctrine, architecture, and hard-won lessons survive the
> move to the new canonical home, **`N0-Gas-Labs/AI-Node-Gate`**. It is *not* a
> claim of live systems, and it contains no credentials or private keys.
>
> Nothing here should be treated as production code. Financial / blockchain
> material is recorded under **security hold** and must not be used with real value.

The founding rule carries forward unchanged:

> **Autonomous nodes propose; a human decides at the gate; every decision is signed and recorded.**

---

## Why this exists

The prior organization accumulated breadth (94 repos, many experiments) but not
always clarity. The recreation package that produced these documents was an act
of **memory, not resurrection**: absorb the capability and the doctrine into one
sovereign hub rather than rebuild ninety repositories. These files are that memory,
kept where the hub itself can hold it.

## How to read this

Read the **charter** and the **portfolio mapping** first — they explain the rules
and what was kept versus left behind. The **status** and **repo inventory** are
reference material: consult them when you need to know what existed, not as a
to-do list. The **roadmap** is prior intent; this hub's own
[`ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md) supersedes it for current work.
